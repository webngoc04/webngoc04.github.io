Well, it's me again. Today I want to touch on a topic that makes me both sad and frustrated every time I think about or type it out: **Is Open Source truly losing its original identity?**

Nowadays, everywhere on tech social media, people buzz endlessly about Open Source. Yet most of the crowd just sits around comparing which repo is "better" than another, or treating Open Source as a free giveaway: *"Oh awesome, they gave this to us for free!"*

What's most depressing is that **the current AI hype wave seems to be replacing and suffocating the purest, most beautiful values of Open Source.**

---

## 1. Original Hacker Spirit vs. Modern Concept Misuse

Looking back a few years (around 2020 and earlier), the concept of Open Source in the eyes of technologists was genuine and pure:
- It represented **freedom in source code**, unburdened by strict terms and proprietary locks from closed-source corporations.
- It stemmed from the **free, autonomous Hacker spirit**, doing everything **FOR THE COMMUNITY**. Developers contributed code out of pure passion for sharing knowledge, building together joyfully without ulterior motives.

What about today?  
Open Source is declared everywhere by everyone. But honestly: Are people opening code to contribute to the community, or primarily for **"Resume Farming"**?

In reality, building personal CV clout and inflating personal branding accounts for the vast majority. Many release repos not for the community, but to show off: *"Look at me, I built this cool thing!"*

> 🚨 **And this mindset directly crushes the founding definition of Open Source:**  
> These repos become nothing more than **free trial products ("Trial Code")**. Creators drop them for public testing, gather feedback to boast *"Look how great I am"*, and then leverage that clout to monetize or promote paid products.  
> 
> If that's the case, **PLEASE STOP CALLING IT OPEN SOURCE. JUST CALL IT 'TRIAL CODE'!**

---

## 2. Repo Recycling: Why Copy-Paste and Headline PR?

Another glaring issue on GitHub today is blatant repo recycling:
- While some projects rise due to genuine quality, countless trending repos are pure **TRASH** or copied code rebranded as original work.
- Every few days, a new repo pops up with identical core logic cloned from another project, wrapped in a slightly tweaked UI, accompanied by clickbait headlines: *"Forget repo X, use my repo instead..."*.

Is this still Open Source? Or is it purely **dirty marketing** for individuals, while the underlying technical core offers zero new innovation or contribution?

AI is inadvertently turning a once-vibrant community into a literal trash pile. Casual users see a shiny UI and smash the Star button, unwittingly becoming free marketing agents for the author.

---

## 3. Production Illusions & Excessive AI Overuse

Looking at the explosion of AI repos today, how many are actually production-ready?

- I'm not saying AI is bad—AI is a powerful tool. But the community is **OVERUSING AI TO AN EXTREME**.
- Thousands of hype-driven projects are spawned overnight, yet hardly any possess the stability, security, or performance required for real production environments. People read about them for hype, but cannot deploy them in actual engineering workflows.

---

## Conclusion: Restoring Dignity to Open Source

Therefore, I sincerely believe: **Channels and individuals hawking the term Open Source for personal PR should just replace it with "Trial Code" already!** Stop using "Open Source" when it has drifted so far from its true essence.

If you genuinely want to understand and honor true **OPEN SOURCE**, look into legendary foundation projects like:
- **`torvalds/linux`** (The OS powering world infrastructure)
- **`gcc`** (The classic compiler infrastructure)
- **`open-quantum-safe/liboqs`** (Open-source quantum-safe cryptography library)

*Those* are the places where the title **OPEN SOURCE** is worn proudly, honorably, and truthfully!
